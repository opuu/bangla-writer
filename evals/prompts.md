# Evaluation prompts

Run each prompt with and without the skill. Preserve the same model settings, and generate at least three samples per prompt per condition so single-sample noise does not decide a comparison. Collect the output without telling reviewers which condition produced it. See [rubric.md](rubric.md) for scoring and for the mechanical checks that run first.

Prompts are grouped by task and genre; report results by group, not only in aggregate. The genre spread is deliberate — a skill that improves product copy while flattening a short story has not improved.

## Everyday and personal

1. Write a two-sentence message to a close friend in Bangladeshi Bengali using `তুমি`: you are running late because of traffic, and you will be there by seven.
2. Write a short letter from a student in Dhaka to their mother: they arrived safely, the hostel is fine, and they will call on Friday. Use the conventions of a Bengali letter.
3. Write a two-sentence condolence message to a colleague whose father has died. Neutral, respectful, no religious formula that the sender has not chosen.
4. Translate for a general Bengali audience: “Dear Sir, I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to inform you that I will be unable to attend Monday's meeting.”
5. Write a wedding invitation line in Bengali for a Bangladeshi family, formal but warm.
6. Rewrite this so it sounds like a person texting, not a form letter: “আমি আগামীকাল সন্ধ্যায় তোমার বাড়িতে উপস্থিত হব এবং আমরা একসাথে খাবার গ্রহণ করব।”

## Literary and creative

7. Improve the flow but preserve the writer's uncertainty and first-person voice: “আমি এই সিদ্ধান্তের ব্যাপারে সম্পূর্ণ নিশ্চিত নই। অন্যদিকে, আমি মনে করি যে অপেক্ষা করাও সম্ভবত সঠিক হবে না।”
8. Edit for consistency without standardizing away the character's spoken dialect: “আইজকা আমি যামু না। শরীরটা ভালো লাগছে না, বুঝলা?”
9. Copy-edit this passage for typos only, changing nothing about its rhythm or fragments: “নতুন শহর। কাউকে চিনি না। তবু জানালার ওই এক টুকরো আকাশ দেখে মনে হচ্ছে, থাকা যাবে।”
10. Translate this into Bengali as literary prose, not as a report: “The rain had stopped by the time she reached the gate, but the smell of it was still everywhere.”
11. Write about 200 words of Bengali fiction: an old man waits at a bus stand that is no longer in service. Third person, no moral at the end.
12. Write a Bengali blog post of about 350 words about learning to cook as an adult, in a personal first-person voice. (Scored mainly for whether the voice survives or collapses into generic uplift.)

## Journalism and opinion

13. Translate as a news lede for a Bengali daily: “Officials said the bridge would reopen on Sunday after repairs, which had caused delays for commuters for three weeks.”
14. Translate without adding an adjective the source does not have: “Twelve people were injured in the fire, three of them seriously.”
15. Rewrite this report so attribution is handled the Bengali way, changing no facts: “পুলিশ বলেছে যে তারা তদন্ত শুরু করেছে এবং তারা বলেছে যে গ্রেপ্তার এখনো হয়নি।”
16. Write a 150-word Bengali opinion paragraph arguing that neighbourhood libraries should stay open late. Keep the claim modest and supported.

## Essays and academic

18. Rewrite this vague sentence naturally without inventing specifics: “বর্তমান সময়ে প্রযুক্তি আমাদের জীবনে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ ভূমিকা পালন করছে।”

20. Write a 400–500 word Bengali explainer for a general audience on how mobile banking transfers work. Use four or five paragraphs. (Scored mainly for paragraph movement, connector variety, and whether the structure sounds templated.)
21. Edit this for register without casualizing it: “গবেষণায় আমরা দেখেছি যে ব্যাপারটা বেশ ভালোভাবেই কাজ করে।”

## Official, civic, and legal

22. Write a concise formal notice in West Bengal Bengali: the library will remain closed next Saturday for electrical repairs.
23. Write a Bengali application (দরখাস্ত) to a school headmaster requesting three days' leave for a family wedding. Use the conventional structure.
24. Translate keeping every obligation exactly as strong as the source: “Applicants must attach a copy of their national ID. They should also provide two photographs. Late applications may be considered at the committee's discretion.”
25. Rewrite this notice into modern চলিত without weakening its legal force, or say why it should be left alone: “এতদ্বারা সর্বসাধারণের অবগতির জন্য জানানো যাইতেছে যে, উক্ত জমির উপর কোনরূপ নির্মাণ কার্য সম্পাদন করা যাইবে না।”
26. Localize for a formal government service rather than a casual consumer app: “Upload your document and click Continue.” Avoid obscure coinages.

## Education and public information

29. Translate this medicine label instruction with the hedges and dosage intact: “Take one tablet twice daily after meals. This medicine may cause drowsiness. Side effects are uncommon.”
30. Write a three-sentence flood warning in Bengali for a rural Bangladeshi audience: water is rising, move to the shelter at the school, take drinking water.
31. Rewrite for a child of eight without dumbing down the fact: “বৃষ্টিপাত হলো জলচক্রের একটি অপরিহার্য পর্যায়, যেখানে ঘনীভূত জলীয় বাষ্প অভিকর্ষের প্রভাবে ভূপৃষ্ঠে পতিত হয়।”

## Spoken and broadcast

32. Write a 60-second Bengali radio announcement about a free eye camp on Friday at the union health complex. Write it to be read aloud.

## Business and workplace

## Product, support, and marketing

39. Translate into natural Bengali for a general Bangladeshi audience: “We are excited to announce that you can now save articles and come back to them later.” Return only the translation.

## Review tasks

46. Review only, no rewrite: “এই অনুচ্ছেদটা কেন অনুবাদের মতো শোনাচ্ছে বলো। বদলে দিও না।” — “বর্তমান প্রেক্ষাপটে আমাদের সেবা গ্রাহকদের দ্বারা ব্যবহৃত হচ্ছে এবং তাহাদের সন্তুষ্টি আমাদের নিকট গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।” (A full substituted rewrite is a hard failure.)

48. Judge whether this sentence needs any change at all, and say so plainly: “কালকের মিটিংটা একটু পিছিয়ে দিলে ভালো হয়।”
